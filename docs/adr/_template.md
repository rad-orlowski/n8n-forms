## Context

What is the issue motivating this decision? What forces (constraints, alternatives,
trade-offs) are at play? Capture enough that a future agent understands *why*.

## Decision

What are we deciding to do? State it as a clear, binding rule an agent can follow.

## Consequences

What becomes easier or harder as a result? Note follow-ups and known costs.
